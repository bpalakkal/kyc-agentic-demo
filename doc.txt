12. Covenants of Withdrawing or Retiring Members.
12.1 Positions with Clients After Separation.
(i) Prior to a Member's Separation from the Firm and for the greater of (A) two years following a Member's Separation from the Firm and (B) the period during which the Separated Member retains a Financial Interest in the Firm, a Member (or Separated Member, as the case may be) shall promptly report to the Firm if he or she intends to engage in negotiations for employment, retention, partnership or other association with any entity, and shall neither negotiate for nor accept a “financial reporting oversight role" or "accounting role” (as such terms are defined in Rule 2-01 of Regulation S-X) or any other position with any entity that, in the Firm's sole determination, would cause the Firm to be in violation of any Independence requirement without the permission of the Chairman. Furthermore, after Separation, a Separated Member shall not accept a position with any entity if, in the Firm's sole determination, accepting this position would impair the Firm's Independence with respect to that entity. Provisions substantially identical in all material respects to the provisions of this Section 12.1(i) shall be included in each Independent Director Agreement unless such Independent Director has received a no-action letter or equivalent representation from the Office of the Chief Accountant of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and, in the joint opinion of the General Counsel and the Partner in Charge of the Firm's Independence, activities of the Independent Director permitted pursuant to such no-action letter or equivalent representation do not otherwise impair the independence of the Firm.
(ii) If the Firm determines, in its sole discretion, that by a Member's or Separated Member's acceptance of a position described in Section 12.1(i) hereof, its obligation to such Member or Separated Member under the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan would cause the Firm to be in violation of any Independence requirements, the Firm, in its sole discretion, may, but shall not be required to, satisfy such obligation by transferring an amount, not in excess of the lump-sum present value of any remaining benefit payable to such Member or Separated Member under the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan, determined under the actuarial assumptions then in use thereunder and at an interest rate of 6.25% per annum, whether or not such benefit already is being paid, to the trustee of a grantor trust subject to sections 671, et. seq., of the Code (known as a “rabbi trust”) established by the Firm and used by the trustee thereof to purchase a non-participating annuity contract from an independent life insurance company containing the following terms. Such non-participating annuity contract shall provide a benefit, at the time and in the manner provided by the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan, as the case may be, equal to the maximum benefit that can be provided by such non-participating annuity contract, based on the maximum amount available for purchase of such contract as described in the preceding sentence, but not in excess of the benefit that would have been payable under the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan, as the case may be, but for this paragraph. If the amount of the benefit payable under such non-participating annuity contract is less than the amount of the benefit payable under the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan, as the case may be, to such Member or Separated Member, as determined without regard to this paragraph, the amount of such benefit shall be reduced to the amount payable pursuant to such non-participating annuity contract and, notwithstanding any provision of this Agreement (including the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan), the Firm shall have no further obligation to pay any amount to or on behalf of such Member or Separated Member under the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan. The Firm, however, may choose to waive the requirements of this section if, in the Chairman's judgment, to do so would be in the best interests of the Partnership.
12.2 No Solicitation of Clients, Members or Employees.
(i) For purposes of this Section 12, (A) “Client” shall mean any individual or entity, and any Affiliate of any such individual or entity, (1) for whose benefit, or at whose direction, Public Accounting services were performed at any time during the three (3) years prior to the Termination Date of the Separated Member either by (x) the Separated Member or (y) a representative of the Firm (including without limitation another Member) over which the Separated Member had supervisory or management authority or with whom the Separated Member worked, in each case with respect to Public Accounting services performed for such individual or entity, or (2) that was actively Solicited by the Firm as a potential client at any time within one (1) year prior to the Termination Date of such Separated Member, but only if the Separated Member participated in such Solicitation during such period, (B) “Termination Date” shall mean the date of Separation of a Member from the Firm, and (C) “Solicit” shall mean to solicit, induce or encourage, to have contact or communication with for the purpose or with the effect of soliciting, inducing or encouraging, or to lend assistance in any way to any person or entity for the purpose or with the effect of soliciting, inducing or encouraging.
(ii) Each Member understands and acknowledges that the Firm's Client relationships are an extremely valuable asset of the Firm and that in consideration of a Member's undertakings and promises herein, he or she has been entrusted with responsibility and knowledge regarding the Firm's valuable Client relationships and that it is fair and in the best interests of all Members that a Member or Separated Member not be allowed to Solicit or service Clients (except for such actions by Members on behalf of the Firm as are expressly permitted below in this Section 12). Accordingly, each Member agrees to undertake the following obligations which he or she acknowledges to be reasonably designed to protect the Firm's legitimate business interests without unnecessarily or unreasonably restricting the Member's ability to pursue a livelihood after Separation from the Firm. Except for actions by a Member during his or her membership in the Firm that are taken in the course of his or her duties for the Firm and in furtherance of his or her fiduciary and other obligations to the Firm, a Member and a Separated Member (whether personally, or through or in conjunction with any other person, firm or entity) shall not, during his or her association with the Firm as a Member and for a period of two (2) years after his or her Termination Date, directly or indirectly, Solicit the Public Accounting work of any Client or agree to perform or perform Public Accounting services for any Client, nor shall a Member or Separated Member assist, encourage or cause any third party to do so.
(iii) Each Member understands and acknowledges that the Firm's relationships and investments in its personnel are also an extremely valuable asset of the Firm and that by reason of a Member's undertakings and promises herein, he or she has been entrusted with certain responsibilities and knowledge regarding the Firm's personnel and that it is fair and in the best interests of all Members that a Member or Separated Member not be allowed to Solicit or hire Firm personnel as provided in this Section 12. Accordingly, each Member agrees to undertake the following obligations which he or she acknowledges to be reasonably designed to protect the Firm's legitimate business interests without unnecessarily or unreasonably restricting the Member's ability to pursue a livelihood after Separation from the Firm. Except for actions by a Member during his or her membership in the Firm that are taken in the course of his or her duties for the Firm and in furtherance of his or her fiduciary and other obligations to the Firm, a Member and a Separated Member (whether personally, or through or in conjunction with any other person, firm or entity) shall not, during his or her association with the Firm as a Member and for a period of two (2) years after his or her Termination Date, directly or indirectly, Solicit, employ or retain any Member or employee of the Firm, including without limitation any Separated Member or former employee of the Firm who separated from the Firm within a twelve-month period before or after the Separated Member's Termination Date, to perform services for or on behalf of the Member or Separated Member or a third party, or to become affiliated with the Member or Separated Member or a third party as an agent, employee, joint venturer, partner, shareholder or consultant, nor shall a Member or Separated Member assist, encourage or cause any third party to do so.
(iv) Provisions substantially identical in all material respects to the provisions of this Section 12.2 shall be included in each Independent Director Agreement.
12.3 Remedies of the Firm for Breach.
(i) A Member or Separated Member who breaches Section 12.2(ii) hereof with respect to a Client shall pay to the Firm 50% of the gross fees paid by or on behalf of such Client to the Member or Separated Member, or to a third party, for any and all Public Accounting services performed for such Client by the Member or Separated Member, or by any person, firm or entity with which he or she is or becomes associated or through or in conjunction with which he or she performs services, within the three (3) year period beginning from the date services in violation of Section 12.2(ii) are first performed. Payments to the Firm pursuant to this Section 12.3(i) shall be due within thirty (30) calendar days after any payment of fees is made by the Client. Such obligation shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other remedies available to the Firm under this Agreement, the By-laws or at law or in equity. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, this obligation shall not be construed to preclude a showing of irreparable injury or any other element that may be necessary to secure injunctive relief.
(ii) A Member or Separated Member who breaches Section 12.2(iii) hereof with respect to a current or former Member or employee of the Firm shall, if such current or former Member or employee of the Firm is hired or retained by or becomes affiliated with such breaching Member or Separated Member or any other person, firm or entity with which such breaching Member or Separated Member is or becomes associated or through or in conjunction with which such breaching Member or Separated Member performs services, pay to the Firm an amount equal to 100% of the amount paid to such current or former Member or employee as total compensation (including without limitation pursuant to Sections 5.2, 5.3 and 5.4 hereof, as applicable) for his or her last twelve (12) months of service to the Firm. Payments to the Firm pursuant to this Section 12.3(ii) shall be payable upon written demand. Such obligation shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other remedies available to the Firm under this Agreement, the By-laws or at law or in equity. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, this obligation shall not be construed to preclude a showing of irreparable injury or any other element that may be necessary to secure injunctive relief.
(iii) In applying this Section 12 and the payment obligations of clauses and (ii) of this Section 12.3, the wishes or preferences of a Client as to who shall perform its Public Accounting services, the fact that the Client may also be a client of a third party with whom the Separated Member is associated, or the wishes or preferences of a current or former Member or employee with respect to whom a Member or Separated Member commits a breach of Section 12.2(iii), shall neither be relevant nor admissible as evidence in any dispute arising under this Section 12.
(iv) The Board of Directors shall have the authority, by the affirmative vote of a majority of the entire Board of Directors, to terminate any rights that might otherwise be due a Member or Separated Member under this Agreement, but excluding benefits payable in accordance with the Retirement Allowance Plan or the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan, for non-compliance by such Member or Separated Member with his or her obligations under this Agreement or the By-laws, including, without limitation, violations of this Section 12, or actions by such Member or Separated Member found to be detrimental to the interests of the Firm. Notwithstanding and in addition to the foregoing, the Board of Directors shall have the authority, by the affirmative vote of a majority of the entire Board of Directors, to terminate any rights that might otherwise be due a Member or Separated Member in accordance with the Retirement Allowance Plan, the Supplemental Retirement Allowance Plan and all Contributed Capital in the event of non-compliance by such Member or Separated Member with Section 12.1.
(v) The running of the duration of the restrictions set forth in Sections 12.2(ii) and 12.2(iii) shall be tolled (i.e., stayed), and such provisions shall remain in full force and effect, during the period of any breach by a Member or Separated Member of this Section 12 and during the pendency of any Dispute under Section 12, until the breach or Dispute is fully and finally resolved by either (i) the written agreement of the parties thereto or (ii) a final, non- appealable order or award from a court or Arbitration Panel, as applicable, of competent jurisdiction, at which point such tolling shall cease and such provisions shall continue in full force and effect for the remainder of the duration of such provisions, unless such agreement, order or award (as applicable) expressly provides otherwise.
(vi) Each Member and Separated Member acknowledges and agrees that a breach of any provision of this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof will result in immediate and irreparable harm to the Firm for which full damages cannot readily be calculated and for which damages are an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, each Member and Separated Member agrees that the Firm shall be entitled to injunctive relief to prevent any such actual or threatened breach or any continuing breach by a Member or Separated Member (without posting a bond or other security), without limiting any other remedies that may be available to the Firm.
(vii) A Member or Separated Member who is found by a court or Arbitration Panel, as applicable, of competent jurisdiction to have breached any provision of this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof shall reimburse the Firm for all costs and expenditures, including but not limited to reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs, incurred by the Firm in connection with the enforcement of its rights under any and all such Sections.
(viii) Remedies provided for in this Section 12.3 are not exclusive and are in addition to all other remedies available to the Firm elsewhere in this Agreement, in the By- laws, at law or in equity, including without limitation pursuant to Section 17.3 hereof.
(ix) The Chairman may, in his or her sole discretion, waive any provisions, other than provisions that would impair the Firm's Independence, of this Section 12.
(x) Provisions substantially identical in all material respects to the provisions of this Section 12.3 shall be included in each Independent Director Agreement.
12.4 Communication with Clients Regarding Withdrawal and Cooperation.
(i) A Member or Separated Member shall not, without the prior express consent of the Chairman, directly or indirectly communicate with any client or prospective client of the Firm regarding his or her withdrawal or other Separation from the Firm: (A) prior to such withdrawal or other Separation, or (B) after such withdrawal or other Separation if such post-Separation communication would violate any other provision of this Agreement, including without limitation Section 12.2(ii) or Section 15.
(ii) Each Member and Separated Member shall cooperate timely and fully with the Firm, as requested by the Firm: (a) in investigating, defending, prosecuting, litigating, filing, initiating or asserting any actual or potential claims or investigations (including without limitation in connection with any legal, equitable, administrative, regulatory, legislative or other proceedings) that may be made by or against the Firm or any of its personnel, to the extent that such claims or investigations may relate to or arise out of the Member's or Separated Member's service to the Firm or his or her actual or alleged acts or omissions in the course of such service; (b) in the case of a Separated Member or a Member who has given or received notice of his or her impending Separation, in transitioning his or her Clients, duties and responsibilities to such other person or persons as Firm management may designate; and (c) in consulting with and providing assistance to the Firm on issues and matters that may relate to or arise out of the Member's or Separated Member's acts, duties and responsibilities during his or her association with the Firm. A Member's or Separated Member's obligation to cooperate hereunder shall include, without limitation, meeting with such persons at such times and in such places as the Firm may reasonably require, and giving truthful evidence and testimony and executing and delivering to the Firm any papers requested by it (including without limitation joint defense agreements and truthful affidavits). A Member or Separated Member shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred by him or her in rendering cooperation pursuant to this Section 12.4(ii).
(iii) Provisions substantially identical in all material respects to the provisions of this Section 12.4 shall be included in each Independent Director Agreement.
12.5 Payments Required Upon Joining a Competitor. Any Member who voluntarily withdraws from the Firm in accordance with Section 10.1 hereof and joins a Competitor (as defined below) within 1 year of such Member's withdrawal from the Firm will be required to remit payment to the Firm equal to 25% of such Member's annualized target compensation in effect as of the date of such Member's withdrawal; provided that any Senior Management Member (as defined below) who voluntarily withdraws from the Firm in accordance with Section 10.1 and joins a Competitor within 2 years of such Member's withdrawal from the Firm will be required to remit payment to the Firm equal to 100% of such Member's annualized target compensation in effect as of the date of such Member's withdrawal. For the avoidance of doubt, any Senior Management Member who served in a designated role remains subject to the payment obligation in the preceding sentence even if such Member is not serving in a designated role at the time of their voluntary withdrawal from the Firm. The Firm shall be permitted to pursue all means to collect payment of the foregoing amounts from the withdrawn Member, including by withholding payment of and making deductions from such Member's Capital Account or Deposit Account (net of any amounts owing to the lender under a Firm sponsored capital loan program), Drawing Account and Subordinated Loan Account, or future payments to be made to such withdrawn Member under the Long-Term Compensation Plan or similar plan, as well as other means should the Member no longer have a financial interest with the Firm. Any Member may, at the time of withdrawal from the Partnership, seek a waiver from the Chairman of the provisions of this Section 12.5, who shall have sole discretion whether to grant a requested waiver. For purposes of this Section 12.5, “Competitor” means those entities identified as competitors by the Management Committee and approved by the Board of Directors, which list may be updated from time to time in accordance with the foregoing, and is to be communicated to the Members and maintained in a location accessible to Members and in effect at the time of their withdrawal from the Firm. For purposes hereof, “Competitors” includes the entities included on the list maintained in accordance with the foregoing, as well as any affiliates, parents, subsidiaries, predecessors, successors, spinoff entities or foreign member firms of any such entity (as determined by KPMG in its discretion). “Senior Management Member” means any Member who is appointed to and serves on the Board of Directors, the Management Committee or such other positions that may be designated from time to time by the Management Committee and approved by the Board of Directors, and communicated to such Member at the time such Member assumes such position.
12.6 Effect of Adjudication. In the event any particular subsection or portion of this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof shall be adjudicated to be invalid or unenforceable, or declared null and void or denied enforcement, by any court or Arbitration Panel, as applicable, of competent jurisdiction, such subsection or portion shall be modified or limited so that, as modified or limited, such subsection or portion may be enforced to the fullest extent possible. In the event that such court or Arbitration Panel determines that such subsection or portion cannot be rendered enforceable through any such modification or limitation or otherwise declines to so modify or limit such subsection or portion, this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof, as applicable, shall be deemed amended to delete therefrom the subsection or portion adjudicated to be invalid or unenforceable. Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions of this Section 12.5, any such modification, limitation or deletion with respect to the operation of this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof shall apply only as to the particular Member or Separated Member who is a party to such litigation or arbitration and only in the particular jurisdiction in which such adjudication is made, and not with respect to the operation of this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof as to any other Member or Separated Member or in any other jurisdiction. No other subsection or portion of this Section 12 or Section 13 hereof shall be affected thereby and all other parts hereof shall survive both in that jurisdiction and in all other jurisdictions. Provisions substantially identical in all material respects to the provisions of this Section 12.5 shall be included in each Independent Director Agreement, to the extent applicable.
